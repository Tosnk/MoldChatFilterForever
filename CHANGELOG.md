# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release for World of Warcraft: Forever
- Hides any chat line containing Cyrillic letters, whole line including the sender
- Optionally hides every line from Cyrillic-named characters, even when written in Latin letters
- Per-source toggles in Escape > Options > AddOns > MoldChatFilterForever: General, Trade, LookingForGroup, LocalDefense and other channels filtered by default; Say, Yell, Emote, Party, Raid, Instance, Guild and Whisper untouched by default
- Your own messages are never hidden
- `/mcff`, `/mff` and `/moldfilter` commands: open options, on/off, status, test, reset
